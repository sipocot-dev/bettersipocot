# Sangguniang Bayan of Sipocot

The Sangguniang Bayan is the legislative body of the Municipality of Sipocot, Camarines Sur. It enacts ordinances and passes resolutions for the welfare of the municipality, with the Vice Mayor as its presiding officer.

## About the Sangguniang Bayan

The council:

- enacts municipal ordinances and passes resolutions
- approves the annual budget and appropriation measures
- adopts local development plans and programs
- exercises oversight over municipal offices and programs

Sessions are open to the public.
